Wipro Limited is a major Indian information technology services company with a long-standing presence in consulting, outsourcing, and digital transformation. The company was founded in 1945 and has grown into a diversified technology organization with operations in over 60 countries. Wipro is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and is widely recognized for its software services, cloud engineering, and cybersecurity offerings. The company serves clients across banking, healthcare, retail, and communications. Wipro’s primary business remains IT services and consulting, supported by a strong engineering and delivery network. Leadership has focused on modernizing internal capabilities while growing areas such as cloud, data, automation, and AI. Wipro has also invested in sustainability and digital workplace solutions. Over the years, the company has built a reputation for large-scale delivery, cost efficiency, and enterprise transformation work for global businesses.
