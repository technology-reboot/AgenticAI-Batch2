Tech Mahindra is a global technology company headquartered in Pune, Maharashtra, and is a leading player in digital transformation and IT services. The company has a strong presence in the telecommunications, manufacturing, healthcare, and financial services sectors. Tech Mahindra serves major clients including British Telecom and AT&T, reflecting its long-standing role in the telecom industry. The firm offers capabilities in cloud, AI, software engineering, cybersecurity, and digital business services. Tech Mahindra has also worked on building next-generation platforms for enterprise customers and expanding into new areas such as automation and analytics. The company’s strategy combines technology expertise with a focus on industry-specific solutions, enabling it to support clients through complex modernization programs. Tech Mahindra’s growth has been shaped by its ability to serve global enterprises with scalable delivery models and a strong engineering workforce.
