Infosys is one of India’s most prominent software services companies and was founded in 1981. The company is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, and has grown into a global provider of consulting, digital transformation, and IT services. Infosys is known for its strong engineering culture and for helping clients across industries adopt cloud, automation, and data-led strategies. Major clients include Goldman Sachs, Apple, and Nike, showing its reach across banking, technology, and retail sectors. The company is led by CEO Salil Parekh, who has guided the firm through a period of digital transformation and large-scale modernization work. Infosys has also expanded into areas such as AI, cybersecurity, enterprise platforms, and business process services. For FY2025, Infosys has provided revenue growth guidance of 3–4% in USD terms, reflecting a cautious but optimistic outlook in a competitive market. The company continues to invest in talent development, sustainability, and long-term partnerships with global enterprises.
