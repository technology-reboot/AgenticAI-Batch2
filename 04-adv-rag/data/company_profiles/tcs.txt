TCS, or Tata Consultancy Services, is a leading Indian information technology services company and a flagship of the Tata Group. Founded in 1968, TCS has built a strong reputation as a global digital transformation partner for enterprises across industries. Its headquarters are in Mumbai, Maharashtra, and it operates from multiple locations around the world. The company offers services in consulting, cloud, cybersecurity, enterprise applications, and business process outsourcing. TCS serves major clients such as BNP Paribas, Marks & Spencer, and Nielsen, and has a strong presence in banking, retail, telecom, and manufacturing. In recent years, TCS has emphasized AI, automation, cloud migration, and digital engineering to help clients modernize operations. Leadership includes CEO K Krithivasan, who took over as CEO in 2024. TCS has also expanded through subsidiaries such as TCS iON for business services and Diligenta for insurance operations. For Q1 FY2025, TCS reported revenue of approximately ₹59,748 crore, reflecting steady growth and broad demand for digital services.
